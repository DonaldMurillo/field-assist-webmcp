# Design context

## Product

Field Assist is a live remote-support tool for an equipment operator, a support expert, and Codex acting through the page's WebMCP tools. The demo must read instantly on a laptop while the operator experience must remain obvious and safe on a phone held in one hand.

## Audience

- Primary: a field operator sharing a rear camera from an iPhone.
- Secondary: a support expert watching the live feed and issuing visual guidance.
- Demo audience: hackathon judges evaluating the product, WebMCP integration, and technical credibility.

## Direction

Calm, field-ready, and precise. It should feel like professional service equipment rather than a generic SaaS dashboard: warm off-white work surfaces, dark graphite live-video areas, safety amber for physical guidance, and restrained blue-green connection states.

## Interaction principles

- The live field view is always the visual anchor.
- Connection state is legible without reading diagnostics.
- The phone experience has one dominant action and large touch targets.
- Visual guidance must stay registered to normalized scene bounds on every viewport.
- Motion is brief and functional, with reduced-motion support.

## Typography and shape

Use a practical humanist system sans stack with compact uppercase telemetry labels and clear sentence-case actions. Corners are modest rather than pill-heavy. Fine rules, not floating card clutter, establish hierarchy.

## Accessibility

Maintain WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, semantic landmarks, live regions for realtime status, and a reduced-motion path.
